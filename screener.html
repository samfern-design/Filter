Market screener filters

The same component, used as a screener's "Add filter criteria" picker — categories are criteria groups (Valuation, Profitability, Dividends…) and the options are individual filters. Pick across groups; the count is the number of active criteria.

Screener

Try: add Market Capitalization under Valuation, switch to Dividends and add Dividend Yield — both persist as active criteria. The chip shows the active-criteria count when open. On mobile, the cross-group search finds a criterion in any group.
