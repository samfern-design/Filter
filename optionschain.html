Options chain filters

A single row of chip filters for the options chain — strikes, near money, liquidity, and contract type. Each opens a dropdown on desktop (or a compact bottom sheet on touch) with a single-select list and, where it makes sense, a Custom… value. Selections apply live.

Try: open Near and pick a strike (or Spot) to re-center the chain; open Max spread and choose Tight (< 5%). Filters with a Custom… button (Near money %, Min volume, Min open interest, Max spread) let you type a value. Strikes, Near and Contract type are presets-only. The header trash clears a filter.
